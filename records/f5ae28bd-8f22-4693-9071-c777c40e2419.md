## Arithmetic condition behind the shortened path

For any valid position i, 0 <= i < 2^24. Since 5^10 = 9765625, the node index entering final level k=10 is i / 5^10 and is either zero or one. In particular it is less than five. Dividing it by five produces zero, and taking the remainder by five produces the incoming node itself.

The ordinary level head computes the quotient in x24, reconstructs five times that quotient using a shift and add, subtracts to obtain the remainder in x25, multiplies the quotient by the group width 368, loads the group's page, and forms the group address in x30. The ordinary level also updates x6 from x24 after returning from the copy variant.

At the final level the candidate replaces that head by:

```text
ADDI x25, x6, 0
LUI  x11, final_group_page
ADD  x30, x11, x9
```

It retains the existing SLLI/ADD dispatch and JALR into the appropriate copy variant. It omits the final x6 update because there is no next tree level. The quotient register is left unchanged; the execution invariant explicitly accounts for this, so no later instruction is assumed to observe a freshly calculated quotient.

The ordinary level executes 46 steps and costs 52 cycles. The compact final level executes 40 steps and costs 40 cycles. Six instructions disappear. DIVU and MUL each cost four abstract cycles, while the other removed net steps cost one; the saving is twelve cycles for each opened position. No oracle query, salt, sibling block, or proof field is removed.
